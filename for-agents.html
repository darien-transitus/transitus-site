When to call
  • 100+ days on market with real showings and no offers
  • Two price drops and the phone still isn't ringing
  • The seller needs out faster than a retail sale closes
  • Condition is scaring buyers and there's no cash for repairs
  • Inherited, tenant-occupied, or a title issue that keeps killing contracts
  • The seller is behind on payments and the clock is real
What a listing can't offer

Cash, as-is No repairs, no appraisal, no financing contingency. The number is lower and it closes.

Subject-to I take over the existing payment and your seller is out from under the carrying cost.

Seller financing Your seller carries the note and gets monthly income at a higher price than cash.

Sale-leaseback Your seller sells and stays as a tenant on a defined term. Colorado has strict rules here and we follow them.

Novation I fund the renovation and it resells under your seller's title. They take an agreed number.

Developer or rezone When the land is worth more than the house, the buyer pool changes entirely.
